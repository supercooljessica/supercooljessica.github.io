Operating Room Display

Completed: July 2026

Description:

My mom helps run a non-profit called Vision Care which travels to third-world countries to perform eye surgery and run training courses for the local doctors. On a recent mission to Tanzania, I noticed that it was very difficult for the doctors to communicate with other staff members who couldn't enter the sterile area of the operating room. I used a Xiao ESP32C3 to host a local web server that anyone could log onto and display a message announcement onto the tablets in the operating room.
