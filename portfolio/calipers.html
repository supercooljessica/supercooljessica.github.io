Human Calipers

Completed: Halloween 2025

Description:

It started as a joke ("we're always losing track of our calipers... why don't you just become one?") and turned into one of my most legendary Halloween costumes yet. Featuring incredibly useful measurement markings from "Moo Deng height at birth" to "world's largest onion," these Vernier calipers won't ever leave your sight again!

(This costume was 100% made from scratch and involved sewing, laser cutting, vinyl cutting, and heat transfer. See the video below for the full process.)
