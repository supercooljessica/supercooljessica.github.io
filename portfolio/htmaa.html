How to Make (Almost) Anything 2022

This class was where I began my maker journey. As one of only three undergrads in my section, I was at first intimidated by the older grad students who already had impressive portfolios and years of industry experience. But as we collaborated together in various group assignments, I grew closer to my classmates and realized we all brought different strengths in our varied backgrounds. Here are some of the highlight moments from this awesome class.
